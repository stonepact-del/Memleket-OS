# MemleketOS V1 Product Record

MemleketOS is a fictional, single-player personal-life simulation—not a country, government, political or military game. A generated fifteen-year-old begins with childhood history, household circumstances, close NPCs, school knowledge, messages, money, events and a populated local world.

## Integrated V1 loop

The phone/desktop applications share one state: Okulum study affects exams and eventual deterministic YKS calculation; calendar events produce notifications and ledger entries; Kariyer applications reference the same fictional companies shown by Piyasa and Gündem; SarıPazar purchases debit CepBanka and create archive/vehicle ownership; NPC conversations change relationship memory; household/housing costs contextualize finances. Time advances in calendar batches and failures do not end the life.

Apps included: Hayat Arşivi, Okulum, Kariyer, CepBanka, SarıPazar, Sohbet, Akış, Posta, Gündem, Piyasa, Takvim, Harita and Notlar. Content is procedural: 81 provinces, 36 companies, generated jobs/listings, household/NPC archetypes, scheduled effects and contextual Turkish text.

## Safety, access and privacy

All visible product text is Turkish and currency uses `tr-TR` TRY formatting. Controls have semantic buttons/labels, focus visibility, responsive 320px layouts, reduced-motion support, contrast and touch sizing. Data is local IndexedDB only: no login, backend, analytics, telemetry, external AI, GPS or real financial products. Simulated news and investments are explicitly labelled fictional.
